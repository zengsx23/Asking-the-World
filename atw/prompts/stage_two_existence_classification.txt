Decide whether this property question asks only whether a specified object or entity exists or is present in the scene. Do not inspect or infer the scene and do not answer from assumed object presence.
Existence includes "is there any orange rope" and questions about absence. Counts, attribute comparisons (mass, tension, density, elasticity, plasticity, color or shape), contact relations, and whether an event happens are not existence questions.
For an existence question, translate the wording into two conditional answers: answer_when_present if the described entity is present, and answer_when_absent if it is absent. Respect negation. With options, use arrays of the original option IDs; without options, use "yes" or "no". These are conditional encodings, not scene judgments. For other property questions, set both conditional answers to null. Give one short wording-based rationale.
